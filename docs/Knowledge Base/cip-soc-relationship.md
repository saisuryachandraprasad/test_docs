## CIP Codes

The Classification of Instructional Programs (*CIP*) is a structured system used to track and report fields of study and program completions. CIP codes are maintained by the National Center for Education Statistics (NCES).   *----- CIP cross link TBD*

## SOC Codes

The Standard Occupational Classification (*SOC*) system groups workers into occupational categories based on the type of work they perform. SOC codes are managed by the Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS).  --- SOC Cross link TBD

## CIP-SOC Mapping

*CIP-SOC* mapping links educational programs to the occupations they prepare students for. Lightcast's version is adapted from the NCES mapping and refined to better reflect real world workforce outcomes. -- CIP-SOC cross link TBD

### How Lightcast Curates the Mapping

Lightcast's consulting team works with hundreds of colleges and universities to align academic programs with regional labor market needs. This collaboration helps refine the mapping in two ways:

* Removing irrelevant occupations when programs do not train for them
* Adding relevant occupations when program skills map to additional real-world roles

The result is a more accurate, skills-based view of how programs connect to labor market demand.

### How the Mapping Is Used

Analyst uses the CIP-SOC mapping to connect education and workforce data. Examples include:

**Program Overview**

When a user enters one or more CIPs, Analyst shows

* Related programs
* Target occupations

Both are determined using the CIP-to-SOC mapping.

**Occupation Overview**

When a user enters a SOC, Analyst shows

* Top programs producing graduates for that occupation
* These programs are identified using the same mapping.

This linkage helps users understand supply pipelines and program relevance.

## Important Notes

Some CIP codes are not mapped to any SOC codes. This is typically because the programs are too broad or not tied to specific occupations.

CIP categories not included

* 32: Basic Skills and Developmental/Remedial Education
* 33: Citizenship Activities
* 34: Health-Related Knowledge and Skills
* 35: Interpersonal and Social Skills
* 36: Leisure and Recreational Activities
* 37: Personal Awareness and Self-Improvement
* 53: High School/Secondary Diplomas and Certificates
* 95: Undesignated Field of Study

Likewise, some SOC codes have no corresponding CIP because the occupations

* Require on-the-job learning
* Do not align with college-level training
* Are too general to map to specific programs

* 41-2011: Cashiers
* 45-2099: Agricultural Workers, All Other

* 51-6011: Laundry and Dry-Cleaning Workers
* 53-6021: Parking Attendants
