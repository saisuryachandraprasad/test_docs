## ISCO Overview

The International Standard Classification of Occupations (ISCO) is an international classification system developed and maintained by the ILO. It organizes jobs into a clearly defined set of groups based on the tasks and duties performed.

ISCO supports

* The compilation of labor-market statistics
* Recruitment and job-matching through employment services
* Management of worker migration across countries
* Development of vocational training programs and career guidance

**ISCO Hierarchy**

ISCO uses a four-level hierarchical structure

* Major Group->1 digit
* Sub-major Group->2 digits
* Minor Group->3 digits
* Unit Group->4 digits

## ESCO

The European Skills, Competences, Qualifications and Occupations (ESCO) taxonomy is a multilingual classification designed for the EU labor market. ESCO extends ISCO by subdividing the four-digit Unit Groups into five-digit occupation codes, offering additional granularity.

At the four-digit level and above, ESCO and ISCO are identical.
