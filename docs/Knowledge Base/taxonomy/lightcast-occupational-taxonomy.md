The ***Lightcast Occupation Taxonomy*** (LOT) has been updated to reflect ongoing changes in the labor market. This release Version 7 or (v7) introduces new occupations, refined relationships, and improved structural consistency to better represent emerging workforce trends. ------ cross link LOT is TBD

## Overview

Version 7 introduces new occupations, expands coverage of emerging fields, and consolidates areas with limited market representation. These updates ensure that the taxonomy remains accurate, relevant, and aligned with real-world labor market developments.

Key enhancements include

* Introduction of new roles in AI and advanced technology disciplines.
* Consolidation of occupations with low posting volume or limited differentiation.
* Updated labels and structures that strengthen clarity and market alignment.

## Availability

Version 7 of LOT is now the default in

* Snowflake database
* Classification API (including classifier functionality)
* Models API
* Postings and profiles datasets

Occupation data in Analyst and other Lightcast web applications has also migrated to v7. Labor Insight users in Australia, New Zealand, and Singapore are now using v7 as well.

## Update Summary

**Occupation Groups**

Three Occupation Group labels have been updated

| Previous Label                    | Previous Label               |
| :-------------------------------- | :--------------------------- |
| Construction Managers             | Construction Administration  |
| Broadcasting and A/V Technicians  | Media Production Technicians |
| Promotional / Commercial Modeling | Modeling                     |

**Occupations**

* 25 new Occupations introduced
  * 7 entirely new concepts
  * 14 promoted from Specialized Occupations
  * 4 created by dividing existing Occupations

**Specialized Occupations**

* 25 new Specialized Occupations
* 4 new concepts formed by dividing existing roles
* 97 Specialized Occupations merged to reduce unnecessary granularity

There are no changes at the Career Area level. To support continuity, 92% of Occupations and 89% of Specialized Occupations remain unchanged.

## Spotlight on Key Additions

Version 7 expands representation in emerging and rapidly evolving fields, as well as critical traditional roles

**AI and Machine Learning**

New occupations including **NLP Engineer**, **ML Engineer**, and **Generative AI Engineer** reflect the growing importance of AI in all sectors. These updates support improved insights into workforce demand, security concerns, and skills preparation.

**Power Systems Engineering**

Power Systems Engineer roles now include more detailed pathways, with skills aligned to trends such as renewable energy adoption and smart grid technologies.

**Cybersecurity Enhancements**

Updated cybersecurity structures reflect increasing demand for specialized security roles, many influenced by the integration of AI technologies.

**Drone Pilot**

Drone Pilot is a new occupation within the **Design**, **Media**, and **Writing Career Area**. This role includes operating and maintaining drones for aerial photography, videography, surveying, environmental monitoring, and media production.

**Terminology Improvements**

Several occupation names have been updated to better match real-world usage and industry standards.

Overall, v7 strengthens the taxonomy’s ability to represent both labor demand (job postings) and labor supply (profiles), while ensuring appropriate coverage of specialized, emerging, and mission critical occupations.

## Maintaining Version 6

Version 6 of the Lightcast Occupation Taxonomy will continue to be maintained until v7 becomes the default across all Lightcast products. After that transition, v6 will remain available without updates until it is fully deprecated later in the year. Additional details will be shared in advance.
