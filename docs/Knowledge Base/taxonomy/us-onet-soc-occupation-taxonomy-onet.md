The Occupational Information Network (***O\*NET***) provides detailed occupation data including required knowledge, skills, and abilities (KSAs), as well as education and training requirements and alternate job titles. Lightcast integrates O\*NET information throughout its tools to enhance occupational analysis and insights. --- cross link TBD

Central to O\*NET is the O\*NET database, which organizes occupational information using the ***O\*NET-SOC taxonomy***. The version currently used by both O\*NET and Lightcast is the 2019 release, which aligns with the ***2018 Standard Occupational Classification (SOC)*** system. The O*NET-SOC taxonomy expands the SOC hierarchy by adding an additional level of granularity. -------- cross link TBD

The 2019 O*NET–SOC taxonomy includes

* 1,016 occupational titles, of which
* 923 are O*NET data-level occupations

These occupations represent finer subdivisions of the 867 SOC codes, which themselves roll up into

* 459 Broad Occupations
* 98 Minor Groups
* 23 Major Groups

O\*NET releases updates to its database twice per year, and Lightcast incorporates these updates into its quarterly dataruns when available. Updates to the O\*NET-SOC taxonomy do not follow a fixed schedule, but Lightcast continually monitors O*NET for new releases.
