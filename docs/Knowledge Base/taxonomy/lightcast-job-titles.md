## Overview

Lightcast maintains a comprehensive library of more than 75,000 job titles. While frameworks such as SOC and O*NET offer structured ways to categorize roles, they do not fully capture the niche, fast-changing nature of jobs in today’s labor market. Lightcast Titles reflect the terminology employers use directly in job postings, giving users a real-time view of new and emerging roles. When used alongside the ***Lightcast Occupation Taxonomy*** and Lightcast Skills, they provide a powerful lens for understanding labor-market dynamics.

In general

* Job Titles represent what a person in a role is called.
* Occupations describe what the individual actually does.
* Skills define the tasks, capabilities, and competencies required to perform the role.

Together, these taxonomies provide a complete and structured view of job function, role identity, and skill expectations.

## Advantages

**Recency**: Lightcast Titles are refreshed every few weeks to keep pace with evolving roles and terminology in the labor market.

**Market Alignment**: Because Lightcast Titles are standardized from the language employers use in their postings, they offer a direct, primary source reflection of how job titles are changing.

## Methodology

### Raw Title Collection

Titles originate as raw titles extracted directly from the job title field of postings or profiles. For example, a Facebook posting may list a position as **Data Science Manager, Messenger**. This unprocessed text becomes the raw title.

### Normalization & Standardization

Although many raw titles are technically unique, they often represent the same underlying role. Variations may occur due to formatting differences, misspellings, spacing, punctuation, acronyms, or industry jargon.

To resolve this, Lightcast analyzes these titles sometimes examining the full context of the job posting to understand the true function of the role. Each raw title is then normalized to the most accurate Lightcast Title.

Continuing the example above, **Data Science Manager, Messenger** would be standardized to **Data Science Manager**.

### Title Assignment

Each posting is tagged with the closest standardized title in the Lightcast system. This ensures consistent representation and enables deeper, more accurate analysis. Lightcast Titles are available across Lightcast tools for direct use in labor-market research and workforce analysis.
