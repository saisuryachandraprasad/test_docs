IPEDS publishes completions data across a range of award and degree levels. While some are self-explanatory, others require additional clarification. The following definitions are adapted from the IPEDS Glossary for the ***Completions component.***

## Associate Degree

An award requiring at least 2 but less than 4 years of full-time equivalent college work.

## Bachelor’s Degree

An award requiring at least 4 but not more than 5 years of full-time equivalent college-level work.
This includes

* Bachelor’s degrees earned through 5-year cooperative work-study programs
* Programs where 4 years of study are completed in 3 years

A cooperative program alternates class attendance with employment in business, industry, or government.

## Certificate

A formal award certifying successful completion of a postsecondary education program.

## Doctor’s Degree 

A doctor’s degree that does not meet the definitions of

* Doctor’s Degree – Research/Scholarship
* Doctor’s Degree – Professional Practice

### Professional Practice

A doctor’s degree awarded upon completion of a program that provides the knowledge and skills required for professional licensure or practice. The total time to degree including pre-professional and professional study must equal at least 6 full-time equivalent academic years.

*Examples*: D.C., D.C.M., D.D.S., D.M.D., J.D., M.D., O.D., D.O., Pharm.D., D.P.M., Pod.D., D.P., D.V.M.

### Research/Scholarship

A Ph.D. or other doctoral award requiring advanced study beyond the master’s level. Programs must include

* Preparation and defense of an original dissertation
* An original project demonstrating significant scholarly or artistic achievement

*Examples*: Ed.D., D.M.A., D.B.A., D.Sc., D.A., D.M., and others.

## Master’s Degree

An award requiring completion of at least 1 but not more than 2 full-time equivalent academic years of study beyond the bachelor’s degree. Some programs such as Theology degrees (M.Div., M.H.L./Rav) may require more than two full-time equivalent academic years.

## Post-Master’s Certificate

An award requiring completion of an organized program beyond the master’s degree that does not meet doctoral-level requirements.

## Postbaccalaureate Certificate

An award requiring completion of an organized program beyond the bachelor’s level. It is intended for individuals who already hold a bachelor’s degree but does not meet the requirements of a master’s degree.

<Callout icon="⚙️">
  Teacher Preparation certificate programs, even when requiring a bachelor’s degree for admission, are classified as sub-baccalaureate undergraduate programs.
</Callout>

## Postsecondary Award, Certificate, or Diploma

Below the baccalaureate level, IPEDS recognizes three duration-based categories

**Less than 1 academic year**

Programs requiring

* Less than 1 academic year (2 semesters or 3 quarters)
* Fewer than 30 semester/trimester credits, 45 quarter credits, or 900 clock hours

**At least 1 but less than 2 academic years**

Programs requiring

* At least 1 but less than 2 full-time equivalent academic years
* 30–59 semester/trimester credits, 45–89 quarter credits, or 900–1,799 clock hours

**At least 2 but less than 4 academic years**

Programs requiring

* At least 2 but less than 4 full-time equivalent academic years
* 60–119 semester/trimester credits, 90–179 quarter credits, or 1,800–3,599 clock hours
