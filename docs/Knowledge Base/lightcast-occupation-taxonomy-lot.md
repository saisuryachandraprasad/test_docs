## Overview

LOT identifies roles that are truly the same across employers, regions, and languages even when job titles differ.
The taxonomy includes 1,900+ occupations organized into four levels:

1. Career Areas
2. Occupation Groups
3. Occupations
4. Specialized Occupations

LOT is used across job postings, profiles, and labor market models to provide a unified view of work.

## Career Areas

These are the broadest categories. They group together large sets of occupations that share the same general discipline or domain helpful for exploring wide career pathways.

## Occupation Groups

These categories bring together occupations with closely related skill sets or role requirements. They reflect **fields** or **disciplines** that students and early-career workers commonly explore.

## Occupations

LOT Occupations represent distinct working roles with defined knowledge, skills, and abilities. They align with national classification systems, undergraduate programs, and typical entry points into the workforce or new industries.

## Specialized Occupations

These are the most granular roles in LOT. They reflect the specific responsibilities, skills, and credentials employers seek for specialized roles or advanced programs such as master’s degrees and certificate pathways.

## Key Benefits

| Benefit    | Description                                                                                                                                              |
| :--------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Granular   | Provides more detail than national taxonomies while still supporting reliable analysis, enables building career ladders using Specialized Occupations.   |
| Specific   | Identifies true roles across employers, regions, and languages, even when job titles vary, helps close gaps created by inconsistent or broad job titles. |
| Global     | Uses a single taxonomy across countries, allowing clear comparisons of roles, skills, and labor trends worldwide.                                        |
| Responsive | Updated annually to stay stable for long-term use while capturing emerging roles as they appear in the labor market.                                     |
