Lightcast provides two technical papers that explain the construction of our Input-Output (I-O) model. These documents are designed for readers who already have a strong understanding of I-O theory and want deeper insight into Lightcast’s methodology.

**Establishing the NAICS 6-Digit U.S. Base Model**

This paper outlines the foundational structure and features of our I-O model, with particular emphasis on the expanded U.S. national I-O technology matrix. A key highlight is its coverage of all ~1,000 NAICS 6-digit industry codes, a level of detail unique to the Lightcast I-O model.

**Lightcast Multi-Regional Social Accounting Matrix (MR-SAM) Modeling System**

This document describes the multi-regional social accounting matrix (SAM) model developed by Lightcast in 2012, which replaced our earlier I-O framework. It details the structure, assumptions, and enhancements introduced through the MR-SAM approach.
