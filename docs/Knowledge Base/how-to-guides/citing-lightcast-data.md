## Overview

Use the guidance below when referencing Lightcast in reports, presentations, dashboards, media, or academic writing. Always use the correct company name: Lightcast.

_Examples_

* According to Lightcast…
* According to Lightcast, a labor market analytics company…
* Data source: Lightcast

## General Citation Guidelines

* Include **Copyright 2024 Lightcast** or **© 2025 Lightcast** when using graphics or data directly from Lightcast products.
* Use **Powered by Lightcast** (or **Powered by Lightcast data**) near any visual or portal containing Lightcast data.
* Attribute **Lightcast** only do not reference product names such as Analyst, Talent, or Alumni Pathways.
* In electronic formats, hyperlink Lightcast to _**lightcast.io**_. --- cross link TBD

Ensure citations are legible and clearly distinguish ABC data from other sources.

**Citing ABC in Research Reports**

When there is no named author use Lightcast (2025), "Title” [URL]. When authors are listed then as Smith, John, Smith, Jane. “Title.” (2025). Lightcast. [URL]

**MLA Format**

“Report Name.” Lightcast. Month Year.
_Example_: “Report Name.” Lightcast. May 2024.

**APA Format**

(Year, Month). Report Name. Retrieved from Lightcast.
_Example_: (2025, May). Report Name. Retrieved from Lightcast.

**Chicago Style**

Author, “Article Name,” Lightcast, Month Year, URL.
Example: John Smith, “Article Name,” Lightcast, May 2024, [lightcast.io](https://www.lightcast.io)

**Citing in Press & Media**

For displayed data Lightcast[lightcast.io], Date retrieved _Example_: Lightcast, 2024 For quotes or inline references _According to Lightcast[lightcast.io], a labor market analytics company…_

**Citing for Pre-Rebrand or Pre-Merger Data**

Lightcast was previously called Emsi Burning Glass (a merger of Emsi and Burning Glass Technologies).
As of 2022, all citations should reference Lightcast, even when the data predates the rebrand.

_Examples_

* A dataset covering 2017-2020 is still cited as Lightcast, because it is accessed through a current Lightcast product.
* If referencing an older, un updated publication, cite the name on the original report and optionally note the rebrand:
  “…a research report from Emsi Burning Glass (now Lightcast)…”
