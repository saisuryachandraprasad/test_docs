## Job Postings

Job postings are updated daily.

## Profiles

Profiles data is sourced from external vendors and updated monthly. Each month, Lightcast receives new and revised profile information and incorporates it into the profiles dataset. Updates are documented in the ***Profiles Changelog***.  ----- cross link TBD

## Classifier Updates

Both job postings and profiles rely on machine-learning classifiers to “tag” information such as

* Skills
* Companies
* Job titles
* Other metadata

These classifiers are human-curated and continuously improved through internal research and user feedback. Updated classifier versions are released monthly.

When a classifier update occurs

* All job postings and profiles are reprocessed
* Counts for skills, companies, job titles, and other classifications may change
* Reports run before and after an update may produce different results due to improved tagging accuracy

Classifier updates are also documented in the Profiles Changelog, including links to individual classifier changelogs and details on updates made.
