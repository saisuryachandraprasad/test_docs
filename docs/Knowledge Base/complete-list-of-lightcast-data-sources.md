## Overview

Lightcast uses a wide range of government, educational, and private data sources to build its labor market datasets.
Below is a summary of the major sources used across the United States, Canada, and the United Kingdom, as well as sources for job postings and social profiles.

## United States Sources

<Accordion title="Department of Commerce">
  **Bureau of Economics Analysis (BEA)**

  * State Personal Income and Employment (SPI)
  * Local Area Personal Income and Employment (LPI)
  * National Income and Product Accounts (NIPA)
  * Annual Input–Output (I-O) Accounts
  * Benchmark I-O Accounts
  * GDP by State

  **Census Bureau**

  * American Community Survey (ACS)
  * County Business Patterns (CBP)
  * ZIP Code Business Patterns (ZBP)
  * Nonemployer Statistics (NES)
  * Quarterly Workforce Indicators (QWI)
  * OnTheMap (OTM)
  * TIGER/Line Files
  * Population Estimates
  * U.S. Population Projections
  * Census 2000 & 2010 Summary Files
  * Census of Government: State & Local Finances
  * Journey-to-Work (JtW)
  * Consumer Expenditures Survey (CEX)
  * Current Population Survey (CPS)
  * Commodity Flow Survey (CFS)
  * LEHD Origin–Destination Employment Statistics (LODES)
    * Origin–Destination (OD)
    * Resident Area Characteristics (RAC)
    * Work Area Characteristics (WAC)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Department of Labor">
  **Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS)**

  * Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages (QCEW)
  * Current Employment Statistics (CES)
  * Local Area Unemployment Statistics (LAUS)
  * National Industry–Occupation Employment Matrix
  * Occupational Employment Statistics (OES)
  * Occupational Education & Training Projections

  **Employment & Training Administration (ETA)**

  * Characteristics of the Insured Unemployed (CIU)

  **ONET (National Center for ONET Development)**

  * O\*NET database (see datarun documentation for version)

  **Wage & Hour Division**

  * Minimum Wage Laws in the United States
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Department of Education">
  * Integrated Postsecondary Education Data System (IPEDS)
  * CIP–SOC Crosswalk
  * Common Core of Data
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Department of Health & Human Services">
  * National Center for Health Statistics (Health, United States)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Postal Service">
  * Address Information Systems (AIS) Products
  * Delivery Statistics
  * 5-Digit ZIP Product
  * City-State Product
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Railroad Retirement Board">
  * U.S. Railroad Retirement Board
  * Annual statistical tables (Retirement & Unemployment Insurance)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Oak Ridge National Laboratory">
  * County-to-County Distance Matrix (Skim Tree)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Private Source">
  * DatabaseUSA business-level data
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="State Sources">
  * Industry projections published by all 50 states
</Accordion>
