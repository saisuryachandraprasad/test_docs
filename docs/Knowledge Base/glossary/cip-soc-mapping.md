The ***CIP-SOC*** mapping links educational programs to the occupations they commonly lead to, helping identify higher-education talent pipelines. Lightcast’s mapping is based on the National Center for Education Statistics (NCES) framework, with additional enhancements to improve usability and alignment with labor-market analysis.

A ***tutorial*** is available for updating the CIP-SOC mapping within Analyst/Developer.
