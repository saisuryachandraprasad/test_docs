Competitive effect measures how much of a region’s job change is due to factors unique to that region its competitive advantage rather than broader national or industry trends. Because it reflects job change that cannot be explained by the ***National Growth Effect*** or the ***Industrial Mix Effect***, competitive effect isolates the portion of employment change attributable to local conditions.

The calculation is -> `Competitive Effect = Actual Change – ***Expected Change***` ----cross link TBD

A positive competitive effect can occur even when regional employment is declining. This indicates that the region is declining less dramatically than national trends would predict.
