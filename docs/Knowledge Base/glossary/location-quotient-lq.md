A Location Quotient (LQ) measures how concentrated a particular characteristic is in a region compared to the national average. LQs can be calculated for

* Industry or occupation employment
* Resident demographics
* Online profiles
* Job postings

By comparing a region’s share to the national share, the LQ highlights what makes that region distinct or unique. For a more detailed explanation, refer to Lightcast’s article on Location Quotient.
