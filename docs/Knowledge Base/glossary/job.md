A job is any position in which an individual provides labor in exchange for monetary compensation. This includes

* Wage and salary employees who work for businesses
* Proprietors who work for themselves

**How Lightcast Reports Jobs**

Lightcast reports employment as annual averages, with one exception

* Extended Proprietors (Class 4): Counted if they operated at any point during the year, based on tax-return records.

Employment figures represent jobs, not individual workers, since one person may hold multiple jobs. Because of limitations in source data

* Full- and part-time jobs are counted equally (no conversion to full-time equivalents).
* Payroll jobs are assigned to the place of work.
* Self-employed and extended proprietors are assigned to their place of residence.
* Unpaid family workers and volunteers are excluded from all Lightcast datasets.
