The Cost of Living (CoL) reflects the amount of money required to maintain a basic standard of living in a specific region. It incorporates typical expenses such as housing, food, taxes, transportation, and other essential goods and services.

CoL is closely tied to regional wage levels, as higher living costs generally correspond to higher compensation requirements.
