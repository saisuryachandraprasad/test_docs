The ***CIP***-***NOC*** mapping links educational programs to target occupations, helping illustrate potential higher education talent pipelines into specific roles. Lightcast’s CIP-NOC mapping is derived from the U.S. National Center for Education Statistics (NCES) CIP-SOC framework, with modifications to ensure alignment with Canadian NOC codes.
