Total Job Postings represents the complete count of online job vacancies before duplicate postings are removed. ***Deduplication*** is the process of identifying multiple versions of the same job advertisement and counting them only once. ---- cross link TBD

* **Total postings**: The number of postings before deduplication
* **Unique postings**: The number of postings after duplicates are removed ------ cross link TBD

*Example*: If a report shows 12 total postings and 2 unique postings, it means that the original 12 postings included 10 duplicates and only 2 distinct job ads.
