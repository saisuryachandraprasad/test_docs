***NACE*** is the official industry classification system used by member states of the European Union. Similar to ***NAICS*** or ***SIC***, NACE provides a standardized hierarchy for categorizing business activities. NACE consists of a four-level structure, with 21 codes at the broadest (1-digit) level. This hierarchy supports consistent reporting, comparison, and analysis of economic activity across EU countries. ---- cross link TBD

EU member states collect and publish statistics based on NACE codes or on national classifications derived from them.
