Labor Market Information (LMI) is reported at two levels

* Traditional (government) LMI 
* Real-time LMI.

Traditional LMI consists of labor-market data collected and published by public agencies such as the Bureau of Labor Statistics, the U.S. Census Bureau, and the Bureau of Economic Analysis. It provides standardized information for established industries and occupations.

Real-time LMI is derived from online job postings and professional profiles. This information reflects current employer and workforce activity and is not governed or standardized by any single entity.
