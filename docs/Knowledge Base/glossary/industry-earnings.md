Lightcast reports industry earnings by place of work and presents them in two components 

* Wages and Salaries
* Supplements

> The sum of these two values is reported as Total Earnings.

## Wages and Salaries

Wages and salaries correspond to earnings reported in the QCEW. According to the BLS, wages and salaries include

* Bonuses, stock options, and severance pay
* Cash value of meals and lodging
* Tips and other gratuities

In some states, employer contributions to certain deferred compensation plans (e.g., 401(k) plans) They exclude employer contributions to

* Social Security and disability insurance
* Health insurance
* Unemployment insurance
* Workers’ compensation
* Private pension and welfare funds

However, employee contributions to these programs as well as withholdings for taxes and union dues are included, even though they are deducted from gross pay.

## Supplements

Supplements come from the BEA’s State and Local Personal Income datasets and consist of Employer contributions to employee pension and insurance funds Examples include contributions to

* Private pension plans
* Publicly administered government retirement plans
* Group health and life insurance plans

Employer and individual contributions to government social insurance These include:

* Employer-paid portions of government social insurance
* Payments by employees, the self-employed, and other individuals participating in these programs
