The Cost of Living Index (COLI) is published annually by C2ER and provides a benchmark for comparing regional living costs across the United States. The index measures how expensive it is to live in one region relative to the national average and to other regions.

COLI is built from six major expense categories:

* Grocery items
* Housing
* Utilities
* Transportation
* Health care
* Miscellaneous goods and services

An index value below 100 indicates that a region has a lower cost of living than the national average. A value above 100 indicates that the region is more expensive.
