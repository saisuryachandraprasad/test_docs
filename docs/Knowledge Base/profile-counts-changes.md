## Overview

Lightcast is expanding its Profiles dataset by integrating data from our recent acquisition of **Rhetorik**. This update increases the number of available profiles across countries and enhances profile richness where additional information is available.

<Callout icon="💡">
  The change will take effect on 4 August 2025.
</Callout>

## What’s Changing

* New profiles will be added to the dataset.
* Existing profiles may gain additional tagged information.
* Coverage and depth will improve across many countries.

## Impact on Customers

**More Skills Tagged per Profile**

Customers will see increases in data tagging.
*Example*: In the United States, the average number of tagged skills per profile will rise from 9 to 11.
