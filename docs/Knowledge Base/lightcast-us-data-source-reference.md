Lightcast uses a broad set of public and private data sources to create comprehensive labor market datasets. The list below summarizes the major U.S. sources included.

<Accordion title="Department of Commerce">
  ## Bureau of Economic Analysis (BEA)

  * State Personal Income and Employment (SPI)
  * Local Area Personal Income and Employment (LPI)
  * National Income and Product Accounts (NIPA)
  * Annual Input-Output (I/O) Accounts
  * Benchmark Input-Output (I/O) Accounts
  * GDP by State

  ## U.S. Census Bureau

  **Business and Employment Data**

  * American Community Survey (ACS)
  * County Business Patterns (CBP)
  * ZIP Code Business Patterns (ZBP)
  * Nonemployer Statistics (NES)
  * Quarterly Workforce Indicators (QWI)
  * OnTheMap (OTM)

  **Geography and Population**

  * TIGER/Line Files (with DM Solutions Group additions)
  * Population Estimates
  * National & State Population Projections
  * Census 2000 and 2010 Summary Files

  **Government, Workforce, and Household Data**

  * State and Local Government Finances
  * Journey-to-Work (JtW)
  * Consumer Expenditure Survey (CEX)
  * Current Population Survey (CPS)
  * Commodity Flow Survey (CFS)
  * Longitudinal Employer-Household Dynamics (LEHD) Origin-Destination Employment Statistics (LODES)
    * Origin-Destination (OD)
    * Resident Area Characteristics (RAC)
    * Work Area Characteristics (WAC)
</Accordion>
