Profile data is self-reported, typically sourced from public social profiles such as LinkedIn, etc.

Because the information is user-provided:

* It may not align with official statistics, such as census data.
* Certain professions (e.g., CEO, Owner) may be over-represented.
* Job titles, employment dates, and locations depend on the accuracy and completeness of the individual’s updates to their profile.
