The Occupation Snapshot report in Developer provides an overview of jobs, earnings, programs, demographics, and related data for a specific occupation. To begin, select a job title and a region (city, county, MSA, state, or nation).
In this example, we examine Shipping occupations in the Memphis MSA.

<Image border={false} />

<Callout icon="⚙️">
  Your region selection affects which data is available. Choose a metropolitan statistical area (MSA) to access the most comprehensive dataset.
</Callout>

<Image border={false} />

At the top of the report, a map highlights the concentration of Shipping jobs by county. Hover over any county to view the exact number of jobs. For example, Shelby County home to Memphis had nearly 3.4K Shipping jobs in 2019, followed by DeSoto County with 2,451.

## Executive Summary

<Image border={false} />

The Executive Summary presents supply and demand indicators by comparing regional and national data.
The Memphis MSA has over 6.6K Shipping jobs. Although demand is lower than supply, it remains strong for a region of this size. Compensation is approximately $4,000 below the national average, which may benefit employers seeking to hire Shipping workers.

## Supply (Jobs)

The Supply section focuses on employment patterns, job types, commuting flows, and workforce diversity. The employment summary shows past, current, and projected data for the region. While national employment for Shipping occupations has declined steadily over the past 20 years, the Memphis MSA has outpaced national trends aside from a notable dip beginning around 2012.

<Image border={false} />

A chart also breaks down Shipping jobs by industry sector. In Memphis

* 20% of Shipping jobs are in Warehousing and Storage
* About 7% are in Delivery Services

<Image border={false} />

## Net Commuters

The commuter map displays net inflows and outflows of workers in the selected occupation. Hover over any highlighted area to view the county and its net commuter count

<Image border={false} />

* Shelby County has both the highest number of incoming and outgoing Shipping commuters.
* Olive Branch (DeSoto County) also ranks high, with 724 incoming commuters daily.

Below the map, two lists show

* Where Talent Lives
* Where Talent Works

<Image border={false} />

Each includes estimated employee counts. Selecting More Detail displays a commuting map specific to the occupation.
In this example, Olive Branch appears at the top of both lists, indicating it both houses and retains a significant number of Shipping workers.

A demographic comparison follows, showing gender, race, and senior worker representation relative to national averages. Racial Diversity is defined as the total of all non-white employment. These regional estimates are scaled from national data.

<Image border={false} />

## Demographic Details

<Image border={false} />

Demographic data for Memphis is organized into four categories

* Age
* Race/Ethnicity
* Gender
* Educational Attainment

Each category displays both the percentage and count of jobs.
The largest share of Shipping jobs is held by workers ages 25–34, while only 0.4% are filled by workers ages 14–18.

* **Available**: County, MSA, state, national
* **Not available**: City, ZIP code, census tract

## Compensation

<Image border={false} />

Compensation data shows regional and national pay for the occupation and allows comparison against the regional cost of living. For employers, median compensation insights can support competitive wage planning when hiring Shipping workers.

## Demand

The Demand section outlines employer activity in the region

* Nearly 230 employers hired Shippers in the past 12 months
* Over 800 unique postings were published
* Postings remained live for about 35 days on average

<Image border={false} />

Hover over the postings graph to see active postings and estimated hires by month. A table below identifies

* Top companies posting Shipping jobs
* Most common job titles

For example, Express posts the most Shipping roles in Memphis, and Shipping and Receiving Clerks is the top job title.

<Image border={false} />

## Graduate Pipeline

<Image border={false} />

This section shows how regional education programs supply talent for the occupation. Memphis has only one relevant program, and no completions were recorded in 2018. Although this might seem concerning relative to the 700 job openings that year, many Shipping occupations do not require a college degree so program output is not a primary constraint.
