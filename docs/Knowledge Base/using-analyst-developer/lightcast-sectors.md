## Overview

Lightcast Sectors highlight specific areas of the labor market that cannot be easily captured through standard industry or occupation classifications. These sectors are created by Lightcast economists and researchers, in partnership with industry experts, to support public research and provide deeper insights into emerging or specialized topics.

> Lightcast Sectors are currently available for U.S. job postings.

**How Sectors Are Applied**

Sectors are assigned only to job postings. They are defined using a combination of

* Industries
* Occupations
* Skills
* Certifications
* Other posting-based attributes

Because these criteria go beyond traditional taxonomies, sectors capture nuance that standard classifications may miss. For example, not every Electrical Engineer works in a green role sector tagging allows Lightcast to distinguish between them based on skills and context.

**Available Lightcast Sectors**

## Green Jobs

Lightcast developed its Green Jobs sector in collaboration with ***WorkingNation*** to understand the national landscape of green work. These insights shaped the ***Green Jobs Now*** reports at both national and state levels. Green Job postings in Analyst are tagged historically, allowing users to explore long term trends.  --- cross link TBD

Green roles fall into three categories

* **Core Green** jobs primarily focused on the green economy
* **Green Enabling** jobs at green firms but not directly tied to green technologies
* **Green Enabled** jobs requiring green skills

> **Potential Green**” roles (those that could become green in the future) are excluded from the product.

## Data Privacy & Protection

This sector covers roles focused on safeguarding and managing data in response to growing regulatory and security demands.

* **Data Protection** focuses on securing information often in alignment with laws such as **GDPR** or the **California Consumer Privacy Act**.
* **Data Privacy** focuses on controlling access to data to prevent misuse, corruption, or unauthorized exposure.

Lightcast developed this sector with input from multiple partner organizations over several years.

* Industry-specific demand

This definition is widely used in academic and policy research by institutions such as Stanford HAI, OECD, and leading economists. The sector is updated regularly, the most recent update occurred July 7, 2025.
