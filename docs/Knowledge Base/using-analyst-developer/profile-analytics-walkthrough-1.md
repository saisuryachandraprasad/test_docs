The Profile Analytics report offers a streamlined way to explore worker profiles. By analyzing occupations, job titles, schools, skills, and related attributes from online profiles, the report provides a clear view of the available talent in any selected region.

## Getting Started

Begin by selecting the data points you want to analyze. These selections act as filters only profiles that match your criteria are included in the report. In the example shown, we search for profiles in the Seattle–Tacoma–Bellevue MSA and refine the results by selecting

* School: University of Washington
* Occupation: Registered Nurse
* Program: Registered Nursing, Nursing Administration

<Image border={false} />

After setting your criteria, select Run. You can adjust any of these data points later by using the filters on the left side of the report. Once the report loads, it first displays the total number of matching profiles. In this scenario, there are over 1.6K profiles of registered nurses who completed a nursing program at UW.

<Image border={false} />

### Geographic Distribution

The map shows where matching profiles are located. Most profiles are concentrated in Seattle, with additional clusters in nearby cities such as Tacoma and Everett.
You can confirm this by reviewing the Top Cities list beneath the map.

**Top Cities**

Of the 1.6K matching profiles:

* Seattle: ~72%
* Tacoma + Everett (combined): 6%

Select **More** to expand the list and view additional cities.

<Image border={false} />

## Explore Profiles

The Explore Profiles section allows you to view real profiles from social and professional networking platforms. Select any of the five profiles shown, or choose Jump to Profiles List to browse more.

Profile details typically include

* Job title and description
* Employer
* Educational background
* Skills listed by the individual

<Image border={false} />

## Top Companies

This section highlights employers that appear most frequently in the matching profiles. For example

* Swedish Health Services: 187 profiles

## Top Job Titles

Among all matching profiles

* Registered Nurse is the most common job title, appearing in 37.8% of profiles.
* Community Health Nurse and Staff Nurse follow at 2.5% and 2.2%, respectively.

<Image border={false} />

## Top Schools

While the University of Washington was selected as a filter, many individuals list multiple educational institutions. As a result

* Seattle University and Shoreline Community College appear in over 100 profiles.
* Bellevue College appears in 99 profiles.
* Everett Community College appears in 76 profiles.

<Image border={false} />

## Top Skills and Qualifications

This section summarizes the skills most frequently listed in matching profiles

* Nursing appears most often, though not universally.
* Basic Life Support appears in over 50% of profiles.
* Patient Safety, ACLS, and CPR each appear in more than 25% of profiles.
