## Overview

The Profile Analytics report provides a fast and intuitive way to explore worker profiles. By analyzing occupations, job titles, schools, skills, and more within online profiles, the report helps you understand the available workforce in any region.

## Starting the Report

Begin by selecting the data points you want to analyze. These selections act as filters only profiles matching the chosen criteria will be included in the report.

In the example below, the search is limited to the Seattle–Tacoma–Bellevue MSA, and further refined by

* School: University of Washington
* Occupation: Registered Nurse
* Program: Registered Nursing, Nursing Administration

Once all desired filters are selected, click Run.

<Image border={false} />

> You can adjust any filters later using the options on the left side of the report.

## Profiles Matching Your Filters

The first result displayed is the total number of profiles that match your criteria. For example, in the Seattle area, there are over 1.6K profiles of registered nurses who attended the selected nursing program at UW.

The accompanying map shows where these individuals are located. As expected, most profiles cluster in Seattle, but nearby cities—such as Tacoma and Everett—also show notable concentrations.

You can confirm these insights using the Top Cities table beneath the map:

Seattle: ~72% of profiles

Tacoma and Everett (combined): ~6%

Click More to view additional cities.

Explore Profiles

The report also allows you to view individual worker profiles sourced from social and professional networking platforms.

Select any of the five profiles shown, or

Click Jump to Profiles List to view the full list.

Each profile includes information such as job title, job description, education history, and listed skills.

Top Companies

This section displays the companies most frequently associated with the matching profiles.

Examples:

Swedish Health Services – appears in 187 profiles

Seattle Children’s Hospital – appears in 118 profiles

Top Job Titles

The most common job titles listed in the filtered profiles include:

Registered Nurse – 37.8%

Community Health Nurse – 2.5%

Staff Nurse – 2.2%

Top Schools

Although the University of Washington was selected as a filter, many individuals list additional institutions—for example:

Community colleges attended before transferring

Graduate programs completed elsewhere

Schools appearing frequently include:

Seattle University – 100+ profiles

Shoreline Community College – 100+ profiles

Bellevue College – 99 profiles

Everett Community College – 76 profiles

Top Skills and Qualifications

This section highlights the most common skills listed in the matching profiles.

Key examples:

Nursing – appears frequently, though not in all profiles

Basic Life Support (BLS) – ~50%

Patient Safety, ACLS, and CPR – each appears in over 25%

These insights help identify essential qualifications and common competencies among the workforce.
