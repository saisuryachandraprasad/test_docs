## Overview

***The Conference Board*** Lightcast Help Wanted OnLine (HWOL) program is a collaborative initiative designed to track trends in employment opportunities across the United States. HWOL serves as a leading indicator of labor-market activity and overall economic conditions. ---- cross link TBD

The dataset is proprietary and provides monthly insights into changes in online job advertisements at the geographic, occupational, industry, and skills levels.

On the Analyst platform, HWOL enables you to:

* Measure changes in the volume of clean, deduplicated online job vacancies
* Access job-ad coverage across all occupations and counties
* Perform independent tabulations down to granular detail

Economists from The Conference Board and Lightcast offer two complementary HWOL products the HWOL Index and the HWOL Publication Series.

## HWOL Index

The HWOL Index tracks monthly changes in advertised online job vacancies and provides a forward-looking measure of labor-market conditions. Because job advertising typically occurs early in the hiring process, the Index helps signal employers’ intentions to expand or reduce staff and adjust output.

The HWOL Index improves upon the historical HWOL Data Series by

* Reducing volatility and non-economic noise
*  Strengthening correlations with local labor-market conditions

Indices are available for

* The Nation
* National 2-digit SOC occupations
* National 2-digit industries
* All states
* All MSAs

Data begins in December 2005.

## HWOL Publication Series

The HWOL Publication Series complements the Index by measuring the number of total and new online job advertisements in the U.S. using the HWOL Data Series. Lightcast collects online job ads in real time from multiple job boards. Ads are

1. Deduplicated across all included sources
2. Aggregated monthly to create the HWOL Data Series
3. Reviewed annually to add new sources or remove unreliable ones

Seasonally adjusted total and new-ads data are available for

* National
* National 2-digit Occupation
* State
* MSA

Additional detailed breakouts are available on the Developer platform. These include

* City-level geography
* 8-digit O*NET occupations
* 5-digit NAICS industries
* Employer-level data
* Skills and job-type variables

> Granular series are not seasonally adjusted.
